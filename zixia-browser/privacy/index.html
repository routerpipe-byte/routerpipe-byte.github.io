Zixia Browser

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 12, 2026
Effective date: July 12, 2026

Zixia Browser is designed to keep core browsing and reading data on your device. This Policy explains what remains local, when information leaves your device at your request, and the choices available to you.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the Zixia Browser Android application and online resources operated for it. Websites, applications, and independent services you access through the browser have their own privacy practices.

2. Information stored on your device

Browsing history, bookmarks, tabs, shortcuts, cookies, site storage, cache, download records, reading progress, annotations, reader settings, scripts, ad-blocking rules, and most preferences are stored locally. Zixia Browser does not automatically upload this local content to our servers.

You can clear browsing data and download records, revoke Android permissions, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app. If device or cloud backup is enabled, the operating system or backup provider may process eligible app data under its own terms.

3. Browsing, permissions, and downloads

  • When you open a website, that website receives ordinary network information such as your IP address, requested URL, browser information, and cookies allowed for that site.
  • Camera, microphone, and location access are used only when you or Android authorizes access for a website or feature. Permissions can be revoked in Android settings.
  • Zixia Browser downloads files only when requested by the user. Downloaded files remain on the device and are not automatically uploaded to us.
  • You can review and revoke the application's granted permissions at any time in Android settings.
  • Wi-Fi book transfer temporarily starts a local server on your device. While it is running, a person on the same network who knows its address may be able to upload a file. Use it only on a trusted network and stop it after use.

4. Optional online features

The following features transmit information only when you enable or actively use them:

  • Translation: selected text, images, or page content may be sent to the translation provider you choose.
  • AI assistant: your prompt and the selected or page content you ask the feature to process are sent to the provider or compatible endpoint you configure. Provider settings and API keys are stored on your device and transmitted to the selected provider only as required to authenticate your requests.
  • Speech: system speech uses the speech service selected on your device. When an online speech provider is selected, the text to be spoken is sent to that provider.
  • Third-party sign-in: when you choose to use third-party sign-in, the information required to complete sign-in is sent to the selected third-party provider and its authentication service. Third-party sign-in does not create a Zixia Browser account; Zixia Browser does not create or store user accounts. The provider processes and retains information under its own privacy policy.
  • Search and sharing: search, image search, sharing, and opening another application contact the destination selected by you.
  • Sponsored links: the home page may display clearly labeled sponsored or promotional links. Zixia Browser does not track impressions or clicks on these links. When you open a link, the destination website processes information under its own privacy policy.

Review the selected provider's privacy policy before sending confidential information. We do not control how independent providers retain or use information.

5. Zixia online resources and server logs

The app may request legal pages, home-page configuration, scripts, filter lists, or other resources from Zixia domains. These requests do not include your local browsing history, bookmarks, reading data, or downloaded files. Web servers may record standard delivery and security logs, such as IP address, time, user agent, requested resource, and response status. Logs are retained only as reasonably necessary for operation, abuse prevention, and security, then deleted or anonymized.

6. Sharing, sale, and security

We do not sell your personal information. Information is disclosed only to provide a feature you request, to the provider selected for that feature, when required by valid law, or to protect users, the service, and legal rights. Our online resources use HTTPS where supported, but no transmission or storage method is completely secure.

7. Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you delete it, clear app data, or uninstall the app, subject to backups controlled by your device or backup provider. Information sent to independent websites and optional providers is retained under their policies. Zixia server logs follow the limited retention described above. Because Zixia Browser does not create user accounts, there is no Zixia Browser account to delete.

8. Children

Zixia Browser is a general-purpose browser and is not directed specifically to children. Children should use it with the supervision of a parent or guardian, who should review websites, downloads, permissions, AI features, and information submitted online.

9. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing; receive a portable copy; withdraw consent; or complain to a data-protection authority. We will not discriminate against users for exercising applicable privacy rights.

10. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy when features or legal requirements change. The revised version and effective date will be published on this page. When required, we will provide an additional notice or request consent for a new use of sensitive information.
