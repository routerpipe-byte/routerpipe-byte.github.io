At 49, Shakira made a triumphant return at the World Cup halftime show, still able to shatter screens with her hip-shaking.#

But in the eyes of many Chinese viewers, there’s a huge bug in this narrative:

She clearly has a standard ‘European white face,’ so why do global media insist on calling her the ‘Latin Queen’? Why isn’t she considered a ‘White singer’?

This question, on the surface, is about a ‘color difference’ in racial identity. But underneath, it’s about Europe’s internal ethnic hierarchy, America’s simplistic ethnic classifications, and an absurd history of ‘what goes around, comes around.’

After all, in the Western world, ‘White’ has never been a monolithic category; it’s always had its own internal rankings.

The most sophisticated hierarchies often use the simplest classifications.

I. A ‘Matching Game’ to Asians, a ‘Food Chain’ to Europeans#

In the eyes of many Asians, as long as someone has fair skin and a high nose bridge, from the UK to Russia, they’re all one big happy family, collectively known as ‘White people.’

But to Europeans themselves, this classification is appallingly crude.

The internal regional hierarchies and ethnic stereotypes within Europe are as dramatic as a major palace intrigue drama.

Northern Europe / Germany:

Emotionless work machines, disciplined, punctual, and rigorous, as if born to give PowerPoint presentations.

Southern Europe (Spain, Italy, Portugal):

Romantic, hedonistic, and laid-back. Their work efficiency depends on whether the sun is shining and if they’ve had a long enough afternoon tea.

Eastern European Slavs:

Poor, crude, and backward. The stereotype is a bottle of vodka in hand, considered Europe’s ‘poor relatives.’

Jews and Arabs:

One group is watched for ‘controlling finance,’ while the other is labeled with ‘crime and conflict.’

The subtext is:

The ‘all people are equal’ proclaimed in Europe is more of a politically correct line. Their daily glances are the real ‘if you’re not in the same circle, don’t force it.’

II. Germanic Centrism: Whoever Controls the Money Printer Defines ‘Purity’#

If you were to rank the psychological status of modern European nations, it would largely be a ‘Germanic-Anglo-Saxon’ one-man show.

With the industrial rise of Britain, Germany, and the Netherlands, they successfully branded themselves as beacons of civilization:

Rational, diligent, and skilled at building institutions.

Meanwhile, the Latin peoples of Southern Europe, no matter how glorious their ancestry, are now stereotyped as ‘sentimental simpletons who only know how to fall in love and go on vacation.’

In certain historical periods, Northern Europeans even considered themselves the only ‘pure-blooded Whites,’ with Southern Europeans and Slavs being ‘inferior Whites.’

No one dares to say this openly today.

But this cultural subconscious, where ’economics determines blood purity,’ is still very much alive.

III. America’s ‘Muddle-Through’ Approach: What Exactly Is the ‘Latino’ Category?#

In the US census and news, we often see the big four categories:

  • White
  • Black
  • Asian
  • Latino / Hispanic

This creates the false impression that:

Latino is a separate race.

But in reality, this is a product of the US government crudely stitching together ‘race’ and ‘cultural origin’ for convenience.

Classification DimensionIncludesThe Real-World Problem
RaceWhite, Black, Indigenous, AsianA division based on biological appearance
EthnicityLatino / HispanicA division based on culture, language, and geography

This leads to a strange phenomenon:

A Latino person can simultaneously be White, Black, or even of Indigenous American descent.

The ‘White people’ mentioned in the American media often specifically refer to:

Non-Hispanic Whites.

As long as you have any connection to Latin America, sorry, you’re asked to sit at the ‘Latino’ table.

IV. Latin America: The World’s Largest ‘Gene Blender’#

So-called ‘Latin America’ has a genetic makeup as complex as a cocktail.

Here you’ll find:

  • Indigenous peoples of the Americas
  • European colonizers
  • Enslaved Africans brought over by force
  • Later waves of immigrants like Lebanese
  • Italians
  • East Asians…

An Argentinian might look completely European.

A Peruvian might have Indigenous features.

A Dominican might have dark skin.

But once they arrive in the United States, this group of people, completely different in appearance and ancestry, are all stuffed into a blind box called Latino simply because they speak Spanish or Portuguese.

Thus, ‘Latino’ has gradually been racialized.

When ‘Latino’ is mentioned, the mainstream narrative automatically conjures images of:

  • Darker skin
  • An English accent
  • Large families
  • Low-wage manual labor

And Shakira, with her ‘high-purity’ face, delivered a resounding slap to this set of stereotypes.

V. Shakira’s Life of Stacked BUFFs: What Is She, Exactly?#

Let’s look at Shakira’s full name:

Shakira Isabel Mebarak Ripoll

Her ancestry and background are a microcosm of global history.

Paternal side:

Lebanese immigrant (Arab descent).

Maternal side:

Descendant of Spanish and Italian immigrants (Southern European Latin descent).

Birthplace:

Colombia (Latin America).

Based on her skin color, she could easily pass for being purely White.

But from the perspective of her cultural identity and musical upbringing, she is a genuine Colombian who grew up immersed in Latin American culture, creating music in Spanish.

Therefore, she is called the ‘Latin Queen’ not because she has a specific ‘Latin look,’ but because she conquered the world with Latin music.

VI. The Ultimate Irony: Yesterday’s ‘Barbarians,’ Today’s ‘Spokespeople for Civilization’#

The most humorous part of European history is the reshuffling of narrative power.

When the ancient Roman Empire (the ancestors of the Latin peoples) was at its zenith, the Romans looked at the Germanic tribes to the north and had only one assessment:

‘Uncouth, crude, and backward Northern barbarians.’

Back then, Latin was the language of nobility and civilization.

However, with the fall of the Roman Empire and the rise of the Germanic and Anglo-Saxon peoples who mastered the printing press of the Industrial Revolution and modern finance, history completed a perfect, dark, and humorous reversal:

The former ‘Northern barbarians’ transformed into the supreme arbiters of rationality and modern civilization;

while the former ‘godfathers of civilization,’ the Southern European Latin peoples, became synonymous with being lazy and backward in their eyes.

Conclusion#

Who is civilized?

Who is barbarian?

Does skin color matter?

Is bloodline pure?

The facts prove:

Whoever controls the armies, the wealth, and the pen that writes history gets to define what is ‘superior.’

The debate over Shakira’s ‘White face’ and ‘Latin identity’ pierces through this very myth of hypocrisy woven from power and prejudice.
